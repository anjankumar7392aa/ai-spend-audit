### FAQ
**1. Do I need to connect my credit card or bank account?**
No. You simply select the tools you currently use from a dropdown and input your team size. We do the math based on current public pricing data.

**2. How does the audit engine work?**
We maintain a live database of every major AI tool's pricing tiers (Cursor, OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub, etc.). Our engine looks for overkill tiering, cross-vendor redundancies (e.g., paying for two writing assistants), and bulk-discount opportunities. 

**3. Why is this tool free?**
We built this to help startups extend their runway. If your audit reveals massive infrastructure spend, we may introduce you to Credex, a platform that provides discounted AI credits. You are under no obligation to use them.

**4. Will you spam my team?**
Absolutely not. You don't even have to give us your email to see your results. We only ask for an email if you want a PDF copy of your report or if you explicitly request a consultation.

**5. How often is the pricing data updated?**
Our pricing database is audited and updated weekly to ensure all calculations reflect the latest vendor changes, API price drops, and new tier introductions.
