# User Interviews

*Note: These interviews were conducted via Twitter DMs and a private Founder Slack group.*

### 1. "M.T.", Technical Co-Founder, Pre-seed SaaS
**Quotes:**
- *"We literally just pay whatever OpenAI bills us every month. I haven't looked at the breakdown since January."*
- *"I forced my two junior devs to use Cursor, but they keep using ChatGPT Plus in the browser anyway. I’m pretty sure I’m paying for both."*
- *"If you told me I could save $200 a month, I’d probably just ignore it. If you told me I was in the bottom 10% of spend efficiency, I'd fix it immediately."*
**Surprising Insight:** 
Founders don't actually care about saving $100-$200/month in absolute terms because it doesn't move the needle on runway. They care about *status* and not feeling stupid. 
**Design Change:** 
This prompted me to add the "Benchmark Mode" concept to the roadmap. Instead of just showing absolute savings, we need to frame it as "You are overspending compared to peers."

### 2. "A.S.", VP Engineering, Series A Agency
**Quotes:**
- *"We have 15 devs. Half use Copilot, half use Cursor. I buy licenses for whatever they ask for."*
- *"Wait, Anthropic has a Team plan? We just have 5 separate Pro accounts expensed to the corporate card."*
- *"I'd use an audit tool, but I'm not giving you my email unless you actually show me the math first. I hate gated PDFs."*
**Surprising Insight:**
Mid-sized agencies have immense shadow IT sprawl for AI tools because individual contributors just expense Pro subscriptions. There is zero centralized procurement for AI yet.
**Design Change:**
Reinforced the decision to *never* gate the core results behind an email capture. The email is strictly for sending a copy of the report and booking a Credex consultation *after* value is proven.

### 3. "J.R.", Solo Founder, Bootstrapped
**Quotes:**
- *"I use Claude API directly via TypingMind. I cancelled my $20/mo subscription and now pay like $3 a month."*
- *"I don't think I overspend. I'm actually trying to figure out how to spend MORE to automate my marketing."*
- *"I wouldn't use this tool for myself, but I'd 100% share it in my indie hacker Discord to show off how lean my stack is."*
**Surprising Insight:**
Highly optimized solo devs won't be Credex customers (their spend is too low), but they are the most likely to share the URL publicly as a badge of honor, driving viral traffic.
**Design Change:**
I heavily optimized the "Shareable URL" Open Graph preview card to highlight when someone has a "100% Optimized" score. This turns non-customers into free marketers.
