# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User
The exact target user is a **Fractional CTO or VP of Engineering** at a Series A/B startup (20-100 employees). They have budget authority but are under pressure from their board/CFO to extend runway, meaning they care about cutting fat but won't tolerate cutting tools that impact developer velocity.

## Trigger Moment
They are looking at their monthly Ramp/Brex statement, seeing "$4,000" under OpenAI and "$2,000" under GitHub Copilot, and Googling:
* "how to track chatgpt enterprise usage"
* "claude team vs pro comparison"
* "is github copilot worth it for whole team"

## Where they hang out
- **X Lists**: Curated lists of Engineering Managers and CTOs.
- **Slack Communities**: Rands Leadership Slack (specifically the `#engineering-managers` and `#finance` channels).
- **Subreddits**: r/ExperiencedDevs, r/SaaS.

## First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)
1. **The "Roast My AI Bill" Thread (X/Twitter):** I will post a thread showing a redacted audit of a startup that was wasting $1,200/mo by paying for both Cursor and Copilot for the same 30 engineers. The CTA will be: "DM me your AI stack and I'll run it through my calculator, or do it yourself here: [Link]".
2. **Cold Outreach to Series A Founders:** Filter Crunchbase for companies that raised Series A in the last 6 months (they have cash but are establishing financial discipline). Send a 2-sentence cold email: "Hey [Name], most startups at your stage overpay by 30% on AI tools because of overlapping seats (e.g. Copilot + Cursor). Built a free 30-second audit tool to check if you are. Worth a look?"
3. **Rands Leadership Slack Drop:** Post the tool in the "deals/tools" channel framing it not as a promotion, but as an open-source calculator for engineering managers to defend their budgets.

## The Unfair Channel
Credex’s existing waitlist or newsletter. Since Credex already sells discounted AI credits, they have a highly targeted audience of people looking for deals. Running a one-off campaign "Find out if you even need these credits" builds massive trust.

## Week-1 Traction
- 500 unique visitors from the X thread and Slack drops.
- 20% completion rate on the form (100 audits generated).
- 15% email capture rate (15 qualified leads for Credex).
