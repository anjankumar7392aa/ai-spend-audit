# Unit Economics & ROI

### 1. Value of a Converted Lead
To determine what a lead is worth to Credex, we estimate the Lifetime Value (LTV) of a converted customer.
- Assuming an average startup using Credex buys infrastructure credits worth $5,000/month.
- Credex's gross margin on these discounted credits is ~15%.
- Net revenue per customer = $750/month.
- Assuming an average retention of 12 months, the **LTV of a converted customer is $9,000**.

### 2. CAC at Each Channel
Based on the GTM plan:
- **Direct Outreach (Twitter/LinkedIn):** $0 paid budget. Time cost of ~2 hours per day to get 10 high-quality leads. Assuming a $50/hr blended rate for marketing effort, CAC = $10/lead.
- **Viral Loop (Shared Audit URLs):** $0 paid budget. 1 in 10 shared audits brings a new user. CAC = $0.
- **Product Hunt Launch:** $0 paid budget. Can drive ~1,000 visitors, netting 100 leads. Fixed time cost of ~10 hours prep. CAC = $5/lead.
*Blended CAC per lead is roughly $5 to $10 initially.*

### 3. Conversion Funnel to Profitability
To ensure this tool is highly profitable given the low CAC:
- **Audit Completed → Email Captured:** 20% conversion rate.
- **Email Captured → Credex Consult Booked:** 10% conversion rate (targeting only high-savings profiles).
- **Consult Booked → Credit Purchase:** 25% close rate.
*End-to-End Conversion:* For every 1,000 visitors who complete an audit, 200 give emails, 20 book consults, and 5 purchase credits. 
Cost to acquire 1,000 visitors (at blended $5/lead for the 200 leads) = $1,000.
Revenue from 5 customers = $45,000 LTV. 
This yields a **45x LTV:CAC ratio**, which is highly profitable and justifies scaling paid acquisition later.

### 4. Path to $1M ARR
To drive $1,000,000 in ARR in 18 months solely from this tool:
- $1M ARR = $83,333 MRR.
- At $750 MRR per customer, we need **111 active customers**.
- Assuming our 0.5% end-to-end conversion rate (5 customers per 1,000 audits), we need **22,200 completed audits**.
- Over 18 months, that is ~1,233 audits per month, or ~40 audits per day.
*Conclusion:* If the shareable URL viral loop achieves a k-factor of even 0.1, driving 40 audits a day is extremely achievable through SEO and organic social sharing, making the $1M ARR target highly realistic.
